Before you enquire

Fair questions. Clear answers.

Every property is different. These are the basics; I can discuss the specifics with you.

What is a company let?

Maroogr would rent your property as a company under an agreed contract. I’m exploring company lets for short-to-mid-term accommodation for vetted professionals and contractors. You should understand and be comfortable with that intended use before agreeing.

What rent and contract length are you offering?

There isn’t a standard figure or term that fits every property. Both depend on the property, local market and practical requirements. If your property looks suitable, I’ll discuss the proposed terms with you clearly before asking you to commit.

Who handles the guests and day-to-day issues?

The proposed agreement would make Maroogr your main point of contact for the arrangement, including its guest operations. Exact maintenance, repairs, utilities and other responsibilities must be specified in the written agreement rather than assumed.

What permissions and checks are needed?

This depends on the property and intended use. Authority to let, lender and freeholder permissions, insurance, planning, licensing and safety requirements may all need checking. Any proposal is subject to these property-specific checks.

Am I committed if I send an enquiry?

No. An enquiry simply starts a conversation. I’ll review the details, explain what I’m looking for and be honest if I don’t think your property is a fit.
