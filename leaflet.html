Understanding Our Company Lets

We rent your property on a long-term basis, pay you a fixed rent every month, and host vetted short- to mid-term guests, primarily working professionals and contractors. You receive a guaranteed income while we handle everything, including all occupier and compliance responsibilities.

How It Works

  1. You grant us a company let agreement for an agreed term (typically 3–5 years).
  2. We pay you a fixed monthly rent, whether the property is occupied or not.
  3. We look after the property, its security, access, and keep it to a high standard.
  4. We deal with maintenance issues up to £300 per issue before involving you.

Who Stays In The Property?

  • Working professionals, contractors and corporate guests on short- to mid-term stays.
  • All guests are vetted, must agree to strict house rules, and are held fully accountable for any damage or rule breaches.
  • Your relationship is with Maroogr only; we manage all occupier arrangements on your behalf.

Benefits For You

Guaranteed Rent

  • A fixed rent, paid on time every month for the full term, regardless of occupancy.
  • No tenant turnover or empty periods affecting your income.

Hands-Off

  • We take on full responsibility for the property, with no day-to-day involvement needed.
  • No calls, no sourcing tradespeople, no managing check-ins or complaints.

Property Looked After

  • We have a direct interest in keeping the property in excellent condition.
  • Regular cleaning and maintenance is handled by us, at our cost.

No Fees

  • You receive the full agreed rent every month, with nothing deducted.
  • No management fees, percentages, or any other deductions.
