Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who handles your information

Maroogr is responsible for the personal information you provide through this website. Dipun Mistry handles property enquiries. You can contact him at dipunm@proton.me or 07983 979 018.

What I collect and why

When you send an enquiry, I collect your name, email address and property postcode. You may also choose to provide your phone number, role and a message, or contact me by email or phone.

I use this information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and assess whether the property may be suitable;
  • arrange and follow up conversations you have requested; and
  • keep appropriate records of enquiries and any resulting business discussions.

I handle this information for these legitimate business purposes and, where relevant, to take steps you request before entering a contract. I do not add enquiry details to a marketing list or sell them.

Who processes it

The website enquiry form is provided by Formspree, which processes and transmits the information you submit. Formspree’s own privacy policy explains how it handles information.

Email, telephone and website providers also process information as needed to provide their services. Information may be processed outside the UK by service providers; appropriate safeguards should apply to international transfers. I do not share your details with other property businesses for their own marketing.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for the conversation and related records. If an enquiry does not lead to a business relationship, I aim to delete it within 12 months. Contractual, financial or legal records may need to be kept longer where required.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access to your personal information, correction or deletion, restriction or an objection to its use, or a portable copy. You can also ask me to stop using your information for a particular purpose.

Contact Dipun to make a request. I may need to confirm your identity before responding. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

Website data and cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Its hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses and requested pages for security and service delivery. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the technical request needed to deliver them. See Google’s privacy policy.

Have a privacy question?

Email dipunm@proton.me. Please do not include sensitive information in the property enquiry form.
